Research

Overview

My research investigates how to formally evaluate the quality of approximation algorithms. My recent work applied an information-theoretic metric to locality-sensitive hashing for sequence alignment. I am broadly interested in the complexity-theoretic limits of approximation and in theories of algorithms that reason under uncertainty and strategic interaction, particularly computational learning theory and algorithmic game theory.
