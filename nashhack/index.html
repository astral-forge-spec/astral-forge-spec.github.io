NashHack rules

The run

You and your partner go through six stages: three dungeons and three dance heats. Defeat The Ackermann to reach the Final, then finish as high as you can. 1st place makes you champions.

#StageTypeWhat's there
1The CryptDungeon4 monsters, 2×2 fights
2QuarterfinalWaltz heat48 bars, cutoff 80; pros and newcomers
3The CatacombsDungeon6 monsters, 3×3 fights
4SemifinalQuickstep heat46 bars, cutoff 85; adds showboats and bulldozers
5The VaultDungeon5 monsters, 3×3 fights, The Ackermann
6FinalViennese Waltz24 bars, no cutoff: you're ranked 1st to 6th

The run ends if any of these happens:

  • your HP reaches 0;
  • your partner's Composure reaches 0 (they leave, and you can't dance alone);
  • you score below the Quarterfinal or Semifinal cutoff (you're not recalled).

Lead or follow

At the start of every run you choose a role, the way you'd pick a species in DCSS. The leader is always the blue @ and the follower always the pink @. Whichever one is you is underlined.

Dungeons

  • You see about 5 tiles around you. Walls stay on the map once seen; monsters only show while they're in sight.
  • Your partner walks one tile behind you, stepping into the tile you just left. Walking into them swaps places.
  • Monsters within 7 steps chase you. Walking into one, or one walking into you, starts a fight.
  • If a monster walks into your partner, it costs 1 Composure and the monster is dazed for 2 turns.
  • Each floor has 1 potion !, and each kill has a 25% chance to drop another.
  • Take the stairs > to go on. The Vault has no stairs: The Ackermann waits at its far end, and defeating it takes you to the Final.

Fights

Every fight is a zero-sum matrix game:

  • The monster secretly commits to a column first, then you pick a row.
  • A +n cell means the monster loses n HP; a −n cell means you lose n HP. There are no zero cells.
  • Every column has at least one + cell, and every row at least one − cell, so no row is safe and no column is hopeless.
RuleDetail
Size2×2 in the Crypt (Strike / Feint vs Lunge / Block); 3×3 afterwards (adds Guard vs Wait).
Cell size1 to 4 damage; 1 to 5 in the Vault.

CodexKilling a kind of monster unlocks its habit for good. The codex is saved in your browser across runs.

FleeingCosts 2 HP, then 3, then 4… on the same floor. The monster is dazed for 2 turns.

Show every habit (spoilers)
  • Uniform Rat: picks a column uniformly at random.
  • Greedy Goblin: always plays the column holding the single cell that is worst for you.
  • Mirror Mimic: plays the column in the same position as the row you picked last round.
  • Bluffing Bandit: plays uniformly at random, then announces a move. Honest bandits announce the true move 80% of the time, liars 20%. Each bandit is honest or a liar at random.
  • Fictitious Phantom: best-responds to how often you've played each row in this fight.
  • Hedge Wraith: multiplicative weights. Columns that would have hurt you more get played more often, so it's exploitable early and close to minimax later.
  • Nash Knight: plays its minimax mixed strategy. Its game is tilted slightly your way, but only the right mix of rows collects that edge.
  • The Ackermann: minimax like the Knight, and every 4 rounds its − cells grow by 1 (instead of enraging). End it quickly.

Heats: the dance floor

The floor is a ring around a roped-off centre. Every couple travels counterclockwise (the line of dance): left along the top, down the left side, right along the bottom, up the right side. The arrows drawn on the rope show the way.

  • Your couple takes two tiles: the leader @ and the follower @. As in Standard, the follower is in front, moving backwards, and can't see where they're going.
  • One bar per turn. Each bar the couple steps one tile in the chosen direction, and the follower ends up one tile further on. Every other couple then moves.
  • Reverse: stepping the opposite way spins the couple in place, so leader and follower swap tiles.
  • Goal: one full lap before the music runs out. The heat ends as soon as you complete the lap.

Playing Follow

Your partner (the computer) leads, and each bar you see their lead above the floor.

Score = 60 × lap fraction (up to 60) + Floorcraft (starts at 20, can't go below 0) + 4 × Connection.

  • Below the cutoff, you aren't recalled and the run ends.
  • Get through a heat with no partner collisions and you gain +1 Composure and +3 HP.

The Final: Viennese Waltz

The fastest dance on the floor, and the hardest to keep safe. All the heat rules above still apply, with these changes:

RuleFinal
Music24 bars for one lap.
Your travelA step straight on carries the couple 2 tiles (each tile is checked for collisions, and you stop at the first one). Turns and sidesteps are still 1 tile.
Other couplesFive pros and two bulldozers move 2 tiles a bar; two showboats move 3 tiles, then pose for a bar. There are no newcomers.
HoldAllowed, but stopping dead costs Floorcraft −2. In Follow, your leader never holds just to prepare a turn.
ResultNo cutoff. Your score is ranked against five rival couples who score about 98, 94, 90, 86 and 81 (each up to 3 higher or lower). Ties go to you.

1st place: Champions. 2nd or 3rd: on the podium. 4th to 6th: finalists. Your best placement is saved in your browser and shown on the title card.
