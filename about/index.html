More About Me

My interest lies in exploring the mathematical limits of what agents can compute and learn. I'm exploring areas like algorithms, complexity theory, computational learning theory and algorithmic game theory. I love to explore ideas in depth, to figure out why a problem exists as well as solving them. If I don't have to worry about anything, I hope to go to a suburban area with only pens, paper, compilers, and infinite time to think about interesting problems.

Though that wouldn't be so realistic as I still love to connect with people. I love talking about happy things and unhappy things with anyone, or go try anything interesting.

Beyond Academics

Beyond academics, I love to play tennis. I try to play at least once a week, and I hope to go to some cool courts when I'm able to. I also love playing jazz piano and I jam in the jazz club. Two of my favorite video game genres are Roguelikes and tower defense.

I recently just started ballroom dancing and it's been a pretty fun game, as it is one of the few opportunities where I can move by body instead of just my brain, regardless of the weather.
